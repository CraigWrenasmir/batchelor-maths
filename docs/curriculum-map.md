Because the school's own documents were empty, **every mapping below is to the Australian Curriculum v9 content descriptions, quoted from the ACARA document, and is my interpretation of a sensible target**. When the school's scope and sequence arrives, each preset should be re-checked against it and the "school section" column filled in. Nothing in this release claims alignment with the school's documents.

## Interpretation choices (not documented requirements)

* The file names suggest composite bands T-1, 2-4 and 5-7. I assumed those are the teaching groups and spread presets across them, with age-neutral presentation so any preset suits any student.
* "AC_MM" was not explained. I have treated the documents as the school's sequencing of the Australian Curriculum.
* Year 8 and above is not covered by the supplied file names. The integer, decimal and "find the rule" presets reach into Year 6 and 7 content and are presented in the same age-neutral way.

## Flags and missing details

1. **The school's scope and sequence is missing.** All mappings are provisional.
2. **Phonics and literacy:** no literacy document was supplied, so no literacy activity was built. A decodable-word activity needs the school's taught grapheme sequence; nothing should be labelled decodable without it.
3. **Road distances** need confirming by the school; sources differ by a few kilometres for some places.
4. **Acknowledgement wording** on the home page names the Kungarakan and Warai peoples, based on NT Government and Wikipedia sources. The school and community should approve or replace this wording before public use.
5. **Secondary students:** no document covered Year 8 and above. The age-neutral design is intended for them, but the content targets stop at Year 7.
