A matching tile total and a matching shape are checked separately. An alternative arrangement is preserved for comparison; later predictions use the growth rule in the shown pattern. Keeping an alternative does not certify that it follows the same rule. For stairs, the new column grows by one at each step rather than adding a constant four tiles.

## Pack the gear

| Content connection | Example opportunity | Limits and next evidence | School section |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AC9MFN04, Foundation:** partition and combine collections up to 10; connect parts with the whole and recognise small quantities | Make 8 across two trays, then show another split. Explain or demonstrate why moving one object between trays leaves the total unchanged. | Use the up-to-5 or up-to-10 range. A correct total alone does not show part–part–whole understanding. Visible small collections can support recognition, but this is not a dedicated subitising assessment. | to confirm |
| **AC9M1N04, Year 1:** add and subtract within 20 using concrete/virtual representations and different strategies | Change a request from 7 to 10 and adjust the existing collection. Compare adding 3 with recounting. Repair a collection with too many or too few. | The app checks a result. Discussion and a physical follow-up are needed to establish the strategy. Offer more than one approach, including concrete materials. | to confirm |

The five stages are make a requested total, find another split, adjust a changed request, repair a supplied collection, and create a partner challenge. Every valid partition is accepted, including an empty tray. The final requested total can be chosen from 1 to the selected maximum. Transferring an object between trays preserves the total.

## Find the way

| Content connection | Example opportunity | Limits and next evidence | School section |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AC9M1SP02, Year 1:** give and follow directions that relocate people or objects within a space | Plan an arrow sequence to a destination; test it; revise it when a block is added; repair a supplied sequence; give a partner a destination challenge. | The object moves on a grid. Up/down/left/right are relative to the display. The activity does not teach relative turns, compass bearings or a person's changing viewpoint. Include physical-space tasks before inferring transfer. | to confirm |

Short routes use a 4 × 4 map; More steps and Changing routes use 5 × 5. Changing routes also requires visiting a round stop. Any permitted route that finishes at the flag and visits a required stop is accepted; the shortest route is an optional discussion. The last stage allows choosing a reachable flag location while preserving the start, blocks and stop.

## Support, classroom evidence and remaining checks

- **Support is separate from challenge and age.** The original three activities offer Full/Light support. Pack/Find offer Visible prompts, Hints on request and Try independently. Pack changes quantity cues, equations and feedback; Find uses fuller prompts in Visible prompts, with the other two choices sharing shorter prompts. Examples and hints remain available.
- **Listen, Show me and Read feedback** can reduce reading and memory demands. They do not teach literacy or phonics. Words such as “altogether”, “different”, “direction” and “step” still need demonstration. Accept reasoning expressed through action, gesture, a familiar language with appropriate classroom support, or the student's usual communication system.
- **Session length is a proposal.** The teacher guide offers a 15–20-minute routine of modelling, practice, comparison and an off-screen check. Engagement, learning impact and transfer have not been established through a classroom trial.
- **Pack/Find content is bounded.** Pack uses three number sequences per range and Find uses four reflected map variants per route setting. Codes select from these variations; they are not an unlimited random question bank. The final stages allow a chosen quantity or destination within existing constraints.
- **School sequence remains missing.** Readable scope-and-sequence files are needed before claiming school-specific alignment. No full year-level programme is supplied, including for secondary students.
- **Local details remain provisional.** Road distances and their starting points require school verification. Identity/acknowledgement wording and any later local images, stories or language should be selected or approved with the school and relevant community contributors. No cultural knowledge or endorsement is implied.
- **Classroom access remains to be checked.** The testing log distinguishes browser evidence from physical iPad, board, voice and network checks. Mathematical success should be interpreted alongside the support used; a revealed answer is supported practice.
